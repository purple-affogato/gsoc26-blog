## GSoC 2026 - Project Intro: Add MPCP & DPCP Support to RTEMS

Happy to share that I'll be contributing to the RTEMS project this summer as a GSoC contributor. My mentors for the summer will be by Kuan-Hsun Chen and Gedare Bloom. 

My project involves implementing two locking protocols, MPCP and DPCP, to RTEMS based on the [work of Junjie et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.06366), and extending the SMP testsuite for these implementations.

My project proposal can be found [here](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1TBTCcUMX4-82RHPCpA059om2bfwpBfmIqGlpVjMWclc/edit?tab=t.0).
